# 🚗 VehicleOrderProject – Executive Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive **Power BI Executive Dashboard** built on vehicle orders data from **1 Jan 2012 to 31 Dec 2017**. It gives a quick view of revenue, quantity sold and total orders, broken down by **Product Line** and **Deal Size** (Small, Medium, Large).

![Executive Dashboard](Executive%20Dashboard.png)

## 📌 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | **$8.29M** ($8,290,886.79) |
| Quantity Sold | **99K units** |
| Total Orders | **307** |

## 📊 Dashboard Components

- **KPI Cards** – Revenue, Quantity Sold and Total Orders.
- **Date Range Slicer** – Filter data between 2012 and 2017.
- **Deal Size Slicer** – Filter by Large, Medium or Small deals.
- **Gauge Chart** – Revenue against the $8.29M scale.
- **Stacked Column Chart** – Quantity Sold by Product Line, split by Deal Size.
- **Treemap** – Revenue share by Product Line and Deal Size.
- **Area/Line Chart** – Monthly Revenue and Quantity Sold trend over the years.
- **Navigation Buttons** – Page navigation and a reset-bookmark button.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Classic Cars** is the top product line, with about 34K units sold (Medium 19.2K, Small 10.3K, Large 4.5K) and about $1.85M in Medium-deal revenue.
- **Vintage Cars** is second, with about 21K units sold and $0.93M Medium and $0.60M Small revenue.
- Other lines by quantity: Motorcycles, Trucks and Buses, Planes, Ships, then **Trains** (lowest).
- **Medium deals** contribute the most quantity and revenue across almost all product lines.
- Revenue and quantity **peaked around 2014** and then declined steadily through 2017.
